    What was caught

    Pasting and copying are blocked, but every attempt is still counted. Grey bars are logged only and cost no strike.

    Flagged games by group

    A flagged game has at least one strike. If one group is flagged much more, its results need a closer look.

    Rules in the game room

    • Copy, cut, paste, drag-and-drop text and one-shot insertions are blocked. Each attempt is a strike.
    • Leaving the game window for 2 seconds or more (alt-tab, another tab or monitor) is a strike. The room is blurred until the player returns.
    • Right-click and developer-tools shortcuts are blocked and logged, with no strike.
    • 3 strikes closes the room and the game is stored as left, with the integrity log attached.

    This runs in the browser, so it deters and documents cheating. It cannot stop someone using a phone or a second computer. Use the "Games to include" filter above to compare results with and without flagged games.
